We Do Not Part

Central argument

The novel follows the consequences, across generations, of the massacre on Jeju Island (April 1948, the Jeju 4.3 Incident). The narrator sinks into the history of a friend's family and digs up a past that still lives in the body and in the way the present is perceived. The book's thesis is never stated outright; it comes through the structure of the story. Trauma doesn't fade with time: time can deepen wounds instead of healing them, so the past acts more and more on the present, until the present stops holding together. The narrator keeps moving between waking and dreaming, present and past, her own story and her friend's. That formal structure is the content: the instability of reality is the direct trace of the trauma.

Examples / Notable passages

  • The narrator takes on the weight of her friend's story and physically walks through the places of the past: someone else's history becomes her own wound
  • The shifts in time aren't decoration: the past breaks into the present with the same physical force as something happening now
  • As in Human Acts, Kang stays strictly with the oppressed, never the oppressors. The reader never finds a justification of the violence, only its trace in the bodies and memories of those who survive

Synthesis

We Do Not Part carries on Han Kang's work on the relationship between collective history and the individual body. Where Human Acts put the reader in front of raw brutality, this novel works on how that brutality lasts: the way later generations carry a history they didn't live through but that shapes them. The form of the novel (fragmented, dreamlike, moving between time frames) isn't a decorative choice of style. It is Kang's main argument: the reality of survivors and their descendants is unstable at its core, and that instability is the violence still going on.

Objections

  • The dreamlike structure can feel too opaque, especially to readers who don't know much about twentieth-century Korean history
  • The slow pace and the gradual immersion ask for active reading: this isn't a novel you can read passively
  • Some see the way it handles trauma as closer to a literary performance of suffering than to a structural analysis; an objection to weigh case by case
