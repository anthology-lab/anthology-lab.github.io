Marguerite Duras ¶

Writing makes us wild. It takes us back to a wildness from before life. And we always recognize it: it's the wildness of the forests, as old as the world. The wildness of the fear of everything, distinct from life itself and inseparable from it. [...] It isn't only writing, the written word, it's the cries of the night animals, everyone's cries, yours and mine, the dogs' cries.

via: Vasco Brondi, Una cosa spirituale (Einaudi, 2025) — Città contemporanee e foreste primitive

Info & reflection: writing as a return to something from before civilization. Not expression but emission.
