Lin Yutang ¶

"The more we justify our beliefs, the more narrow-minded we become."

Info & reflection: It turns around the common idea that arguing for a position makes it more solid and more open to discussion. Instead, every round of justification adds a brick to the defence and narrows the gaps: you end up defending not the idea any more but what you have invested in defending it.

Attribution: uncertain. Quote collections credit The Importance of Living (1937); not checked against the book.
