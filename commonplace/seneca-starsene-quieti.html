Seneca ¶

Nothing, however, will help you so much as keeping still—talking very little with others, and as much as may be with yourself.

Info & reflection: The maxim packs everything into a reversal: the most necessary words are the ones you say to yourself, not to others. Keeping still isn't passivity: it's the condition in which inner dialogue becomes possible. Seneca insists on a practice, not an abstraction: less noise outside, more clarity inside. It's the stoic foundation of self-knowledge.
