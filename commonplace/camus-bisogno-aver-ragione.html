Albert Camus ¶

"The need to be right is the sign of a vulgar mind."

Info & reflection: Camus ties vulgarity not to a lack of culture but to a psychological need: whoever must always be right gives away an insecurity they mistake for strength. It's an implicit criticism of a certain way of arguing, the one that looks for victory instead of truth.
