anonymous ¶

the art of detachment.
nothing belongs to me.
it's all an experience.

Info & reflection: three lines to explain the modern wish for "experiences". Nothing but a way to numb yourself, to detach from reality.
The real point of view about this should be spiritual. If nothing is mine, losing it is the end of an experience, not a theft. It sits close to Luke 12:15, "life is not measured by how much you own", and to the Stoic line between what depends on us and what doesn't.
