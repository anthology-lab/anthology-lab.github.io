Anthony Bloom

"God can save the sinner you are, but not the saint you pretend to be."

Anthony Bloom (Metropolitan Anthony of Sourozh, 1914–2003) — Russian Orthodox bishop, spiritual writer.

Context: God cannot work with a facade. If you wear a mask of perfection (the "saint you pretend to be"), you leave no room for grace to heal the actual areas of your life that need change.
