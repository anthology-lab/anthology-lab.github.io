Addiction is proof that you're capable of intense devotion. You are just serving the wrong god.

The same energy you waste on porn, dopamine hits, scrolling, weed, toxic relationships, or overthinking — that fire is your greatest weapon. The difference between a slave and a king is not the capacity for obsession. It's what you choose to obsess over. Master your focus. Channel your addiction into discipline. Serve the right god.

Info & reflection: addiction as energy pointed the wrong way, not as a lack of strength: the problem isn't the capacity for obsession, it's the object. It connects straight to the note on doomscrolling: same neurological mechanism, different direction.
