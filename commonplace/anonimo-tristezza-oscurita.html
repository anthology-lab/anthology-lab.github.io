Jorge Luis Borges ¶

I can give you my loneliness,

my darkness, the hunger of my heart;

I am trying to bribe you with uncertainty,

with danger, with defeat.

via: Vasco Brondi, Una cosa spirituale (Einaudi, 2025) — Notizie di me

Info & reflection: offering your own darkness as a gift. Not the light, not the strength.

Attribution: Jorge Luis Borges, "Two English Poems" II (1934), written in English. Brondi quotes it in Italian without the author's name, with "sadness" where Borges wrote "loneliness".
