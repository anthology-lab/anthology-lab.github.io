Alan Watts ¶

Most of us would have rather money than tangible wealth. And a great occasion is somehow spoiled for us unless photographed. And to read about it the next day in the newspaper is oddly more fun for us than the original event.

Info & reflection: Three concrete, sharp examples of confusing the map with the territory: we prefer the symbol to the thing (money vs real wealth), the picture to the experience (photo vs moment), the news to the event. Photography saw this problem coming, and social media has made it even sharper.
