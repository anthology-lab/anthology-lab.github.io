Enzo Celli ¶

Il sé non si trova. Si narra. E ogni narrazione è provvisoria, non nel senso di falsa, ma nel senso di aperta al capitolo successivo.

The self is not found. It is narrated. And every narration is provisional, not in the sense of false, but in the sense of open to the next chapter.

Info & reflection: It solves the problem at the root: the question "who am I?" is badly put if it expects a final answer. Identity is not a hidden object to discover but a narrative process, always under construction. Every answer is provisional and open: not false, only incomplete.
