film

The Madison

What it's about

The story of a New York family unfolds in the wake of a tragedy, as they come to terms with their grief.
Preston Clyburn suddenly dies with his brother during a vacation in rural Montana, leaving his family exploring the bonds between people in the midst of deep sorrow.

First reaction

The beautiful landscapes and the truth of feelings. It all seemed very real and relatable, unlike many other films and tv series that usually display a fake version of life.

What it triggered

Grief is a topic which for me is a medium in this series, a moment in which everything comes to surface gifting a huge opportunity: dealing with problems.
You can decide to bury them again, or face them.
All characters in the series have to face life changing decisions: Stacy will go back to NY or move to Montana? Abigail will finally own her life, try to explore the future with Van? Paige, unemployed, in the middle of a nervous breakdown, how will she deal with it?

Free notes

Stunning performances, especially from Michelle Pfeiffer (Stacy), Kurt Russell (Preston) and Beau Garrett (Abigail).
