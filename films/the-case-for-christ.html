The Case for Christ

What it's about

Based on Lee Strobel's book of the same name (1998). In 1980 Lee Strobel, an investigative journalist and declared atheist, uses his skills as a journalist and legal reporter to try to take apart his wife Leslie's new Christian faith, which opens a rift in their marriage. After almost two years investigating the historical evidence for the resurrection, he finds evidence he didn't expect, and with it a new faith.
